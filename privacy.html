PRIVACY
POLICY

Last updated: May 14, 2026

GL Hybrid Engine ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Team Lopes Training, based in ZTN, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and your rights as a subscriber or visitor.

By using this website or subscribing to any of our programs, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have questions, contact us at glopes0712@gmail.com.

01. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to deliver our services. This includes:

  • Name and email address — provided when you submit the private coaching inquiry form or complete a Stripe checkout.
  • Payment information — processed exclusively by Stripe. We never see, store, or handle your raw card details.
  • Inquiry content — messages sent through the private coaching contact form, including your goal and training background.
  • Usage data — standard web analytics such as browser type, pages visited, and referring URL, collected anonymously.

We do not collect health or biometric data through this website.

02. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively for:

  • Processing and managing your subscription via Stripe
  • Sending you a welcome email and your TrainHeroic invite link after successful payment
  • Responding to private coaching inquiries submitted through the contact form
  • Delivering ongoing subscription-related communications (billing updates, program changes)
  • Improving the website based on anonymous usage patterns

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

03. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate this platform. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Stripe — handles all payment processing and subscription management. Your card data is stored and processed solely by Stripe under PCI DSS compliance. stripe.com/privacy
  • Formspree — receives private coaching inquiry submissions and forwards them to our email. No account creation required from you. formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy
  • TrainHeroic — delivers your daily workouts via their athlete app. You will create your own account upon receiving the invite. trainheroic.com/privacy
  • Zapier — automates the welcome email workflow between Stripe and our email system. Data passes through Zapier servers temporarily. zapier.com/privacy

04. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period thereafter for accounting and legal purposes. Inquiry form data is kept until the inquiry is resolved or for a maximum of 12 months.

Stripe maintains its own retention schedule for payment records, which may exceed this period for regulatory compliance.

05. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Cancel your subscription at any time — subscriptions are month-to-month with no lock-in period

To exercise any of these rights, email us at glopes0712@gmail.com. We will respond within 14 business days.

06. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies. Essential browser functionality (such as scroll position and form state) may use sessionStorage, which is never transmitted to our servers.
