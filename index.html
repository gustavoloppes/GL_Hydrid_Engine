PRIVATE COACHING

GL HYBRID ENGINE
RUN COACHING

"Your paces aren't copied from someone else's plan — they're calculated from your own fitness, and they get sharper as you get faster."

Built on Critical Velocity (CV) — not generic pace charts. Every athlete starts with a 5-minute all-out CV test, establishing their personal threshold speed. Every workout is then calculated as a precise percentage of that athlete's CV.

CV Test First
5-minute all-out effort sets your exact personal threshold — no borrowed pace charts.
Individual Zones
Zones 1 through 5c calculated from your CV — two athletes on the same day run completely different correct paces.
Science-Based Periodization
Issurin block periodization, Daniels-Gilbert VDOT, and critical-velocity research — not arbitrary effort labels.
TrainingPeaks Delivery
Pace zones built into your athlete profile. As your CV improves, your entire zone structure recalculates automatically.
